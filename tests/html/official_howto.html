Link Aadhaar

Last updated on 12 March 2026

Overview

This service is available to all individual taxpayers who hold a Permanent Account Number and an Aadhaar number. Taxpayers can link their Aadhaar with their PAN using the e-filing portal, both before logging in and after logging in. The service validates the details entered against the records held by the Unique Identification Authority of India before the link is created.

Prerequisites

You need a valid PAN, a valid Aadhaar number and a mobile number that is registered with Aadhaar so that the one-time password can be delivered. If the name, date of birth or gender in the two records do not match, correct the record held by the respective authority first, and then try again after the correction has been processed.

Step-by-step guide

Step 1: Go to the e-filing portal homepage and select Link Aadhaar under Quick Links. Step 2: Enter your PAN and Aadhaar number and click Validate. Step 3: If a fee is applicable, pay it through the e-Pay Tax service before continuing. Step 4: Enter the six-digit one-time password received on the mobile number registered with Aadhaar and click Validate to submit the request.

After submission, the status of the request is shown on the screen. It can take a few days for the link to be confirmed. You can check the status at any time with the Link Aadhaar Status service, which needs only the PAN and the Aadhaar number, and does not require you to log in to the portal.
